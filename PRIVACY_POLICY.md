# Privacy Policy — Instagram Creator Toolkit

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Instagram Creator Toolkit generates caption ideas, hashtags, keywords, and descriptions from the topic the user types into the extension. The topic and generated text are processed locally in the extension; they are not sent to the extension developer.

## Optional trend feature
When the user clicks “Load current trends”, the extension requests Google's public Google Trends RSS feed for India (`https://trends.google.com/trending/rss?geo=IN`). This request goes to Google and may include normal network metadata handled by Google. The extension does not send the user's topic, Instagram account details, login credentials, browsing history, or generated captions in that request.

## Data collection and sharing
The extension developer does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data. The extension does not ask for Instagram credentials and does not read Instagram pages. It does not automate posting, likes, comments, follows, or account creation.

## Permissions
The extension requests access to the Google Trends host only to retrieve trend suggestions when the user clicks the trend button. It does not require broad access to all websites.
